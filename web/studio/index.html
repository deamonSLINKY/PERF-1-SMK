What a piece holds, and what it cannot hold yet

The studio edits only what the FM-1 keeps with a piece, so nothing is lost on the way to the unit:

  • Drums: six patterns of six lanes, sixteen steps; each lane's step tricks (ratchet, push, level), one set a lane.
  • Bass: sixteen steps (rest, note, accent, glide), each a scale step above the chord's root and an octave; played when the bass line's mode is Steps.
  • Acid and pluck: a sixteen-step line each, kept when frozen (acid: scale steps above the chord with octave, accent, slide; pluck: rungs of the chord's ladder and a level).
  • Chords: eight progression slots (a degree, 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars, a chord type each), played in turn, or the genre's walk.
  • Every setting of the piece (tempo, key, mode, levels, sounds, the FILTERS and SCREAM pages).

The firmware would need new stored fields for: lines longer than one bar (bass, acid, pluck are one-bar shapes that follow the chords); step tricks for each pattern (now one set a lane, shared by all six); the pad's own notes or voicings for each chord; automation of a setting over time; an order of patterns of your own (Song walks the piece's patterns by itself). A MIDI file's longer parts will be cut to these shapes until then.
