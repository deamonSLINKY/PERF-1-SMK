## Description (paste as is; the chapter lines make YouTube's chapters)

PERF-1 0.17.0 is out: free, open-source live-performance firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1 (GPL-3.0). Everything since the last video, over one take of one disco piece (DISCO 03), made from the firmware itself in its browser emulator: what you hear is what the FM-1 plays. Then the three new genres, and the web pages: the Library, MIDI learn, the Install page.

Install (Chrome or Edge, the FM-1 on USB): https://deamonslinky.github.io/PERF-1/install/
The Library (share pieces, play other people's): https://deamonslinky.github.io/PERF-1/pieces/
Manual, browser emulator, editor: https://deamonslinky.github.io/PERF-1/
Source and release notes: https://github.com/deamonSLINKY/PERF-1/releases/tag/v0.17.0

New since 0.15: pieces move through their own drum patterns with fills (Song or Loop). The chords move round the circle of fifths, with secondary dominants (E7 A7 D7 G7 C) and borrowed chords, no diminished chord by accident, and phrases that end at home. THEORY draws it like a theory book: the chord, the next one and the arrow between. Your own progressions: eight slots, a chord type for each, eleven ready fills on the black keys, and Pedal. Bass steps: sixteen steps that follow the chords. Filter types and SCREAM, saved with each piece. Punch-in FILTER, DELAY and PHASE on the tracks you pick. Auto FX. A set list. Industrial, psytrance and dark forest. It powers on where you left off. Other gear plays it straight over USB (an MPC, an MPK mini, a Model:Samples), and any MIDI knob can turn any setting. Newer FM-1s (the new flash chip) work, and the Install page's Full speed undoes M-VAVE V16's 12% slowdown.

Thank you, sincerely, for the awesome feedback, the comments and the ideas: THEORY, the fills, Pedal, bass steps, SCREAM, the punch-ins, the new genres and the newer-chip fix all started as your messages. It makes me want to keep pushing harder. Keep the suggestions coming: issues on GitHub, the Firmware Hub, the comments below.
