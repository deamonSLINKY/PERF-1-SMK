The pads light in the mode's colour, white while held; MIX shows the mutes red and the solos white. Velocity 30 at the softest (the SMK's factory pad curve starts high).

FOLLOWERS (FOLLOW pads)

Drones on the chord, for playing over (Steven's idea, issue #3): the pad's four voices become four followers, low and sustained. The pad stops playing its own chords while they are on; FOLLOWERS off gives it back.

F1 rootC2 .. B2. Hold (the default): the key's root the whole time, the chords moving above it; or Follow each chord's root
F2 third, F3 fifthC3 .. B4, the chord's third and fifth. Common (the default): a note the next chord also has stays, otherwise the nearest of its own; Follow: always its own; Hold: the key's own chord
F4 colourthe chord's 7th (the default), 9th, 6th, sus4 or octave
Evolveoff, slow (every 4 chords), faster (every 2): the colour changes by itself, in the scale, mostly 7ths and 9ths
StyleSwell (sustained, breathing slowly), Pulse (the notes again at the rate, 1/4, 1/8, dotted 1/8 or 1/16, a slow attack), Drift (8 bars of each)
Registerlow, mid, high (an octave each)
Glide0, 0.4, 0.8, 1.4, 2 s between notes

The pad's level (fader A4), its sound (PADTONE), attack and release (Pad env) and every effect act on them. The bottom row's fills write the progression and it plays at once. Kept with the piece: SAVE stores them, another piece has its own.

CALL AND RESPONSE (FOLLOW pads, third page)

Another instrument calls and PERF answers (Steven's idea, issue #3: a guitar through a 1010music Bento, whose MIDI out sends a note for each phrase, or a keyboard). Notes on the Call channel (channel 2 at first; the pad next to CALL steps it 1-16) are calls: they play no PERF voice. A call starts with its first note and ends a beat after the last one is let go. Connect the other instrument over USB (the Bento as a USB host, with the SMK on its USB port) or through a computer.

AnswerWhat PERF does
Harmony (the first)each called note, snapped to the scale, becomes F4's for the rest of the chord: the followers harmonise the call (F4 sounds it even when it's off)
Echowhen the call is over, the acid plays it back in key from the next beat: up a fifth, then an octave up, mirrored, reversed, in turn. A new call stops it
Steereach call moves the chord a fifth down round the circle (I IV vii iii vi ii V I); a call starting on the key's root takes it home
Swelleach called note swells the followers: up over a beat, back over two

Leave room (on or off): while a call plays, the pluck and the acid go 40% down, so the call sits on top. Harmony and Swell act on the followers, so turn those on too. The Call settings are the player's (not the piece's) and come back after a power-on.

Install and the way back

Install from the PERF-1 SMK page (Chrome or Edge on a computer, a data USB cable, M-UPGRADE closed). The way back to M-Vave's firmware: the same page with M-Vave's SMK-37 Pro 016 file (m-vave.com, PC Firmware), or M-UPGRADE. Installs leave the keyboard's start-up code and its factory key, pad and wheel calibration alone. KNOB BANK held at power-on opens the hardware test (KNOB BANK + FADER BANK for 2 s leaves it); the bench plan is SMK37-HWTEST.md in the source.
